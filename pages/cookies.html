Cookie Policy

Biscoon does not use tracking, advertising, or analytics cookies. This page explains exactly what we store in your browser, and why. Last updated 2026.

We don't set cookies

Biscoon.com does not use HTTP cookies at all — not for tracking, not for advertising, and not even for keeping you signed in. There's no cookie consent banner on this site because there's nothing to consent to: we have no non-essential cookies to ask permission for.

What we store instead

Signing in and a few preferences are handled through your browser's local storage, which stays on your device and is never sent to advertisers or shared with third parties. Specifically:

  • Sign-in session — a login token so you stay signed in between visits, and a timestamp used to sign you out automatically after a period of inactivity.
  • Display preferences — small things like whether you last viewed listings as a grid or a list, or whether you collapsed a sidebar.
  • Temporary caches — weather, currency exchange rates, and your approximate city (used to show nearby listings and discussions first) are cached briefly in your browser tab so pages load faster. These clear automatically when you close the tab.

You can clear all of this at any time through your browser's site settings, or by clearing your browsing data — doing so simply signs you out and resets your preferences to default.

Your approximate location

To show weather for your area and prioritize nearby listings and discussions, Biscoon looks up an approximate city from your IP address using a third-party geolocation service. This never pinpoints your exact address, and if it fails or you'd rather not use it, the site still works normally — it just won't rank content by location.

Third-party resources

Biscoon loads fonts from Google Fonts. Loading these font files means your browser makes a request to Google's servers, which — like any web request — includes your IP address. We don't use Google Analytics, advertising pixels, or any other third-party tracking script on this site.

Changes to this policy

If that ever changes — for example, if we add analytics to understand how the site is used — we'll update this page first, and add a consent banner if the law requires one for what we add.

Questions

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@biscoon.com. See also our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.
