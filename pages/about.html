About us

The Global Nepali Ecosystem

Biscoon.com™ connects 4.5 million Nepalis across 90+ countries through community, commerce, and culture — one platform for jobs, housing, marketplace listings, events, and the conversations that keep the diaspora close to home.

What we're building

Nepalis moving abroad — for study, work, or family — have always rebuilt the same things from scratch in every new city: where to find a room, who's hiring, which grocery store carries the right spices, where the community gathers for Dashain. Biscoon brings all of that into one place, organized by category and searchable by location, so that knowledge doesn't have to be relearned by every new arrival.

What's on the platform

  • Jobs & Immigration — Nepali-friendly job listings alongside OPT, H1B, TPS, and green card guidance.
  • Housing — apartments, rooms, paying-guest listings, and real estate, searchable by city.
  • Marketplace — Kinbech classifieds, services, and restaurant & business directories.
  • Community — events, student organizations, matchmaking, discussions, and Nepali news from both home and the diaspora.
