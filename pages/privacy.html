Privacy Policy

This page explains what information Biscoon Inc. ("Biscoon", "we") collects when you use Biscoon.com, and how we use it. Last updated 2026.

Information you provide

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password. When you post a listing, send a message, or update your profile, we store the content you submit — including any photos you upload.

Information collected automatically

We use your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and remember preferences (such as a collapsed sidebar or dismissed notice). If you allow it, we use your device's location to show local weather and default your search radius — you can decline this and enter a location manually instead.

How we use your information

  • To operate the features you use — job and housing search, listings, messaging, and notifications.
  • To keep the platform safe — moderating reported listings and content, and preventing abuse.
  • To contact you about your account or activity on the platform, such as a message from another user.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Sharing

Your public profile and listings are visible to other Biscoon users, as intended. We share data only with the infrastructure providers we use to run the platform (such as hosting and email delivery), and only as needed to provide the service.

Your choices

You can edit or delete your listings and profile information from your account at any time. To request a copy of your data or full account deletion, email support@biscoon.com.

Questions

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at support@biscoon.com.
